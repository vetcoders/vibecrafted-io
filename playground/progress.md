Original prompt: Build a VibeCraft Pipeline visualization as an interactive canvas game/animation.
Each phase of the framework (Init, Workflow, Agents, Marbles, Overflow, Followup, Prune, Hydrate, Decorate, Ship)
maps to a distinct animation phase with marbles being thrown at a board with grooves.

## Phase map
1. BAM — board slams onto canvas
2. Grooves reveal — shape carves out spirally from center
3. First marbles — few, graceful, precise
4. Marbles ramp — handfuls, chaos grows
5. Overflow — too many, >100%, pile up everywhere
6. ERR — freeze, red pulse, "P0" flash
7. Shake — followup quality gate, loose marbles fall
8. Prune — excess drops off, gaps revealed
9. Hydrate — surgical placement of last pieces
10. Decorate — colors saturate, glow appears
11. Ship — golden shimmer, fade, new shape, repeat
